# Product Context

## Register
Brand — personal portfolio / professional website where the design is part of the credibility signal.

## Audience
Professionals seeking networking, collaboration, or industry connections: fellow engineers, potential employers, partners, conference organizers, and technical decision-makers. They scan quickly and value clear credibility signals.

## Purpose
Present Carlos Paramio as a thoughtful Software & Cloud Architect with deep engineering, architecture, and leadership experience. The site should make it easy to understand expertise, evaluate fit, and continue to social/professional channels.

## Brand Personality
Professional, approachable, technically credible, and grounded. A trusted peer rather than a salesperson. The design should feel crafted and calm, not flashy.

## Aesthetic Direction
Clean, confident, and earth-toned. Forest green and terracotta reinforce eco-conscious, scalable technology values. A signature cloud/network SVG graphic provides a distinctive infrastructure motif.

## Anti-references
- Purple/blue SaaS gradients.
- Glassmorphism or frosted cards.
- Decorative shadows and glossy depth effects.
- Emoji iconography.
- Generic AI landing-page patterns.

## Design Principles
1. Earth tones over AI defaults: forest green, green-tinted neutrals, and terracotta accents.
2. Typography as hierarchy: clear scale, readable prose, fluid display headings.
3. Motion with purpose: subtle entrance animations, smooth page transitions, and reduced-motion support.
4. Visual signature: network/cloud infrastructure motif, not generic abstract blobs.
5. Accessibility always: semantic HTML, keyboard support, contrast, ARIA where needed.
6. Light mode primary: dark mode is available but should not drive the identity.
