## Design Context

### Users
Professionals seeking networking, collaboration, or industry connections. Visitors may be fellow engineers, potential employers, partners, or conference organizers. They scan quickly and value credibility signals.

### Brand Personality
Professional, approachable, technically credible. A trusted peer rather than a salesperson. The design conveys thoughtfulness and craftsmanship - "made with care" without being flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean, confident, and grounded. Earth tones palette (forest green + terracotta) reinforces eco-conscious tech values (scalable = energy efficient). A signature SVG network graphic in the hero represents cloud infrastructure. View transitions between pages create smooth, cinematic navigation.

### Design Principles
1. **Earth tones, not AI slop**: Green (#) and terracotta accents, never purple-blue gradients
2. **Typography as hierarchy**: Plus Jakarta Sans, clear scale, fluid headings
3. **Motion with purpose**: Subtle entrance animations, smooth page transitions, theme toggle slider
4. **Visual signature**: SVG network graphic represents cloud infrastructure
5. **Accessibility always**: Reduced motion support, proper ARIA, keyboard navigation
6. **Light mode primary**: Dark mode available via toggle, not forced
